# PodeTelar v0.2.1

## Camera sharing on Windows

- Click **Share camera** to start with your default camera. PodeTelar still
  remembers a device you selected in Camera settings.
- The Camera settings panel shows live video as you choose a device. Your own
  camera view in the room is live too, including while you share your screen.
- Both local camera views are mirrored horizontally. Viewers see the camera's
  original orientation.
- The camera refresh button has a clearer icon.

The empty-room monitor and message are now centered in the stage, and the
monitor has an even outline.
