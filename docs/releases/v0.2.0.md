# PodeTelar v0.2.0

## A redesigned studio

- Choose Grid, Spotlight or Focus to arrange the room around what you’re
  watching. Spotlight keeps one stream large and the remaining previews beside
  it.
- PodeTelar follows your system’s light or dark appearance by default. You can
  choose a fixed theme in Settings.
- Hover over a watched stream for playback controls and its received codec and
  bitrate. The room menu and participant list show who’s connected.
- The redesigned web dashboard shows upload and download traffic for the server
  and each PC. Administrators can also copy the Discord bot’s install link from
  the dashboard.

## Camera sharing and stream quality

- On Windows, share a camera alongside your screen or on its own. The separate
  camera and screen controls let you configure each source independently.
- Preview your camera before sharing, then choose a device, resolution and
  frame rate. The app chooses a bitrate for the codec, resolution and frame rate
  by default. Advanced camera settings let you override it.
- Administrators can set bitrate ceilings and Automatic bitrate targets for
  screen and camera. Shared resolution and frame-rate limits still apply.
- Windows audio playback can recover after the output device reports an
  unsupported format.

## Install and update

Use the built-in updater or download Setup for Windows or the disk image for
Apple Silicon. Camera capture is currently available on Windows. Windows
packages are unsigned and macOS packages use ad hoc signing.
